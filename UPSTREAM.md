## Immutability policy

No experiment in this repository may modify files inside the upstream
submodule. Dataset conversion, configuration, launchers, instrumentation,
statistics, and visualizations belong outside `upstream/`.

Updating CA-SAM requires a deliberate submodule revision update and a separate
commit documenting why the experimental baseline changed.
